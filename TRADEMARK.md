# Trademark Policy

"JobFoundry" is the name of this project. This policy explains what you may
and may not do with the name and logo. It is modeled on community-friendly
brand policies (PostgreSQL, Mozilla, GitLab): protect users from confusion,
allow everything else.

## You may (no permission needed)

- Refer to JobFoundry by name to describe it, review it, or explain that
  your product works with it ("compatible with JobFoundry", "provider pack
  for JobFoundry", "import from / export to JobFoundry").
- Use the name in talk titles, blog posts, tutorials, and academic papers.
- Fork the **code** under the AGPL-3.0 license. Forks are explicitly
  welcome.

## You may not (without written permission)

- Present your product, service, or fork **as** JobFoundry, or use the name
  or logo in a way that implies official status, endorsement, or
  maintainership ("JobFoundry Pro", "Official JobFoundry Cloud",
  "JobFoundry by Acme").
- Use the name or logo in your product's name, logo, domain, or app-store
  listing. A fork must be renamed (e.g. "Acme Job Board, based on
  JobFoundry") with a clear statement that it is unofficial and where to
  find the original.

## Why this exists

Jobseekers trusting this tool with their resumes need to know exactly whose
software they are running. Confusion about provenance is a safety issue, not
a branding exercise.

## Questions and permissions

Open an issue titled "Trademark request: …" or email
[security@covai.org](mailto:security@covai.org). Community and
non-commercial uses are routinely approved; we only ask for a clear
"unofficial / based on JobFoundry" label.
