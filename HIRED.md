# Hired with JobFoundry

JobFoundry hasn't launched yet — so this wall is empty on purpose. It exists
as a promise about what kind of proof matters here.

## What will go here

When jobseekers land roles using JobFoundry, their stories go here: what
they were looking for, which parts of the pipeline helped (capture, scoring,
tailoring, tracking), and what they'd tell the next person. Each entry links
to a public issue or discussion the person wrote themselves — never
solicited quotes, never anonymized marketing copy.

## Ground rules (for future us)

- **One ask per hire, respect the answer.** If someone shares a win in a
  discussion, maintainers may ask once whether they'd like it on the wall.
  No means no; silence means no.
- **Privacy first.** No real names, employers, or numbers unless the person
  publishes them first. Links point to what _they_ wrote.
- **Honest scope.** A story says JobFoundry _helped_, never that it _got_
  someone hired. People get themselves hired.
